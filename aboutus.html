Crafting Memories in Geelong

Welcome to Once Upon a Memory Designs. What started as a passion for personalized creations has grown into a dedicated custom event signage and decor studio based in Geelong, VIC.

Whether it's the excitement of a wedding, the joy of a new baby announcement, or honoring a loved one with a beautiful memorial, I believe that every milestone deserves a touch of personalized magic. From whiskey glasses for the groomsmen to large-scale acrylic event backdrops, every piece is handcrafted with precision and care.

We don't just make signs; we create keepsakes that tell your unique story.
