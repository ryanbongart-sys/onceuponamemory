Our Story ❤ | Once Upon a Memory Designs

Welcome to Once Upon a Memory Designs. We create custom signage and personalised pieces designed to make your special moments feel truly unforgettable. What started as a maturnity leave passion for personalized creations has grown into a dedicated custom event signage and equipment hire company based in Geelong, VIC.

Whether it's the excitement of a wedding, the joy of a new baby announcement, or honouring a loved one with a beautiful memorial, I believe that every milestone deserves a touch of personalized magic. From large-scale acrylic event backdrops to champayne glasses for the bridesmaides every piece is handcrafted with precision and care. Not to mention our luxurious pastel soft play equipment available to entertain the children of any event.

Created during a special season of life, our business is built on love, creativity, and attention to detail.
